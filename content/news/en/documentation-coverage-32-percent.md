The current-track catalog now has public evidence for 1,145 of 3,534 cells, or 32.4%. That is 524 more sourced cells than the research pack's 621-cell baseline.

The latest research adds exact consumer-web evidence for Microsoft Copilot, Grok, Gemini, Perplexity, and Replit Agent; release-pinned reviews of Continue, Cline, and Zed; and a broad documentation review of Amp CLI. Product boundaries, plan gates, protocol revisions, and known source contradictions remain attached to the affected cells.

This is documentation coverage, not a product score or usage share. No harness was installed, signed into, or run for these claims. Cells without sufficient public evidence remain unknown, and limitations stay visible as partial support.
