The atomic current-track catalog now has assessed statuses with direct reviewed evidence for 1,328 of 3,534 cells, or 37.6%. That is 707 more reviewed cells than the research pack's 621-cell baseline.

The latest reconciliation integrates every accepted Microsoft Copilot and Gemini web finding and every responsible Claude Desktop finding from the completed research briefs. Claude Desktop gains exact-surface records for files, code execution, sandbox networking, approvals, MCP, credential storage, sharing, privacy controls, Skills, and subagent delegation. Ambiguous claims remain qualified; MCP stdio is explicitly marked as a provisional editorial inference rather than direct transport wording.

The site also separates support status from assessment basis and confidence. Readers can open each matrix cell to inspect its conditions and sources, then submit a bounded correction with the exact product surface, source URLs, a private-by-default explanation, and a trackable public receipt. Coverage measures catalog completeness, not product quality, popularity, or usage share.
