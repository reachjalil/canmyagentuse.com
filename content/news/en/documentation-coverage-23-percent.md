The current-track catalog now has public evidence for 827 of 3,534 cells, or 23.4%. That is 206 more sourced cells than the research pack's 621-cell baseline.

The newest cycle adds Agent Skills evidence across exact CLI, web, and desktop surfaces; a revision-pinned review of Goose CLI's MCP implementation; and exact-surface MCP Apps records for Cursor and OpenWork Desktop.

This is documentation coverage, not a product score or usage share. No harness was installed or run for these claims. Cells without sufficient public evidence remain unknown, and limitations stay visible as partial support.
