Can My Agent Use helps people choose services their agents can actually work with. We publish the account requirements, interfaces, permissions, and human handoffs behind each answer. Sponsorship supports this research and its public availability.

## Founding sponsor: OpenWork

**Sponsored placement.** <a href="https://openworklabs.com/" rel="sponsored noopener">OpenWork</a> is our first confirmed founding sponsor. Its placement is advertising, not evidence of compatibility or a recommendation that it is the best agent for every task.

OpenWork is also a subject of the existing harness research. Readers can inspect the [OpenWork Desktop record](/harnesses/openwork-desktop) and its evidence using the same criteria as every other harness. Sponsorship does not change that record’s findings.

## What sponsors can buy

- **Founding sponsorship:** a clearly labeled site placement and acknowledgement on this page.
- **Category sponsorship:** an advertising placement next to a relevant category, visually distinct from the comparison.
- **Research underwriting:** funding for a stated question or report, with the funding disclosed and findings determined by the evidence.
- **Sponsored demonstrations:** a labeled demonstration with its environment, setup, limitations, and observed outcome disclosed.

Availability, duration, deliverables, and pricing are agreed in a written scope. We do not publish invented audience numbers, guarantee traffic, or promise favorable research outcomes.

## What money does not change

Our product comparison follows the reader’s category and interface requirements. A payment cannot buy an “official” integration label, a higher support status, suppressed limitations, removal of alternatives, or editorial veto. Sponsors can submit evidence and factual corrections through the same process as anyone else.

Research coverage and tested support are different. Funding an investigation does not mean the product passed a live test. A provider announcement, documentation review, community implementation, and observed transaction retain their own evidence labels.

## Clear advertising

Paid placements say **Sponsored** next to the sponsor name. Sponsored outbound links carry `rel="sponsored"`. If we add affiliate links, their commercial relationship will be disclosed near the relevant link. Ads do not impersonate search results or account-connection controls.

We use a direct, static placement for the founding sponsor. This placement adds no third-party ad network script or cross-site ad tracking.

## Discuss a sponsorship

Use a [sponsorship inquiry on our public repository](https://github.com/reachjalil/canmyagentuse.com/issues/new?title=Sponsorship%20inquiry) to start a conversation. Include your organization, the audience or category you want to reach, the proposed period, and the deliverable you want to fund. Keep confidential commercial information out of the public inquiry.
