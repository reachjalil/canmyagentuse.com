## What happens next

We review your public evidence, check the exact product and interface, and decide whether to add a guide or request more information. A submission does not publish a listing or award a support status. Sponsorship is handled [separately](/sponsors) and cannot buy a favorable finding.

Your receipt records the submission's review status. Contact information stays private. The form defaults to keeping your submitted explanation internal; editors use public evidence when writing catalog entries. Do not submit passwords, API keys, customer data, or private documentation.

## What to include

- **App name and public HTTPS website.** Identify the service you want reviewed.
- **One useful agent task.** For example, create a note, deploy a site, or connect an existing account.
- **A public HTTPS source.** Link to developer documentation, a maintained integration, or a specific help page that establishes the capability.
- **Optional affiliation and contact permission.** You can submit without providing an email address.

Review the [privacy policy](/privacy) and [research methodology](/methodology).
