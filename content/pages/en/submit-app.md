## Start with four simple details

- **App name.** The exact product you want reviewed.
- **Public HTTPS website.** Where people can learn about it.
- **One useful task.** A sentence describing what someone wants an agent to do.
- **One public HTTPS starting link.** Official docs, an integration README, API reference, or `llms.txt`.

You do not need to write your own compatibility report, implement MCP, or create an `llms.txt` file before submitting. Optional notes can point us to setup, permissions, pricing, versions, and known limits. Email and affiliation are optional; contact requires your permission.

## What our research agents look for

Our research uses agents to read public documentation, follow relevant sources, and draft an assessment of how the app presents its capabilities. Editorial review checks the evidence and scope before a finding is published. This is an agent-assisted research process, not an instant scan triggered by the form.

We investigate four practical steps: **sign up, connect, buy or subscribe, and get work done**. We assess **MCP tools, MCP Apps, APIs, CLIs, and local-file access separately**. One interface does not establish support for another. We record setup, permissions, human handoffs, restrictions, and evidence dates.

## Follow your receipt

The form records your submission in the editorial queue and returns a receipt. Save it to follow review status and requests for more information. Review may lead to a new guide, an update, a request for evidence, or a decision not to list. There is no promised turnaround or automatic publication.

We distinguish documentation review from live tests. Submitting a link does not authorize account access, installation, purchases, or actions in your app. Unverified details stay visible as unknown; a well-written submission does not earn a favorable status. [Sponsorship](/sponsors) is separate from findings.

Your notes and contact information stay private. Editors cite public evidence in published guides. Never send passwords, API keys, customer data, or private documentation.
