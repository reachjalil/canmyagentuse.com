Can My Agent Use does not ask you to create an account and does not collect votes, support reports, search queries, or form submissions. Search and comparison interactions run in your browser. The site does not currently use first-party behavioral analytics, advertising trackers, or marketing pixels.

The public catalog is hosted on Cloudflare Workers. Like other hosting providers, Cloudflare may process ordinary request metadata needed to deliver and protect the service, such as IP address, user agent, requested URL, timing, and security signals, under Cloudflare's applicable terms and privacy documentation.

Outbound evidence links take you to third-party sites. Their privacy practices apply after you leave this catalog.

If collection is added later, this page and the product UI must be updated before launch to explain the data, purpose, retention, and controls.
