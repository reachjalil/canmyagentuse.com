A feature can be technically supported but unsuitable for a workload because its quota is shared, its rate limit is low, or an interrupted session cannot resume. This catalog grouping keeps those common questions visible without inventing an umbrella compatibility claim.

Useful evidence attaches every amount and limit to a plan, model or mode, organization scope, reset period, client, execution environment, and review date. It distinguishes a documented maximum from an observed one and records what happens at the boundary.
