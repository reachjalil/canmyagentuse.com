A feature can be technically supported but operationally unusable for a workload because its cost is opaque, its quota is shared, its run times out, or a disconnected client loses all progress. This family makes those day-two questions visible.

Useful evidence attaches every amount and limit to a plan, billing region and currency, model or mode, organization scope, reset period, client, execution environment, and review date. It distinguishes a documented maximum from an observed one and records what happens at the boundary.
