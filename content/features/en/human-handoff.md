This row covers an accountable transfer of responsibility, not merely alerting someone that a run exists. A useful handoff identifies the recipient or queue, current status, requested decision or action, relevant context and artifacts, deadline, permissions, and who owns the next step.

Evidence should record assignment and reassignment, acknowledgement, comments, escalation, service-level timers, notification channels, redaction, audit events, resume semantics after a decision, and whether the agent can continue autonomously while the handoff is pending.
