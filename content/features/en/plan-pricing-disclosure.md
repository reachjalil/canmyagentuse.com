This row asks whether a prospective operator can determine what the exact harness costs and which plan unlocks the evaluated behavior. A headline monthly price is incomplete when model premiums, tool use, hosted compute, storage, connectors, sub-agents, or overages are billed separately.

Evidence should record billing unit and period, included quota, variable rates, hard and soft caps, seat minimums, annual commitments, currency and tax treatment, trial behavior, enterprise-only terms, and the effective date. The catalog does not convert those facts into a synthetic “value” score.
